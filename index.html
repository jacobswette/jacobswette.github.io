Review before you use it

ZangFu generates a suggested draft — not a final note. The output is clinically informed, but TCM is complex and errors can occur.

Always verify before charting: Chinese characters for less common points · Herb dosages · ICD-10 code accuracy · Any clinical finding the AI may have inferred but you didn't state

Your clinical judgment is the final authority. The note is a starting point, not a finished product.

FAQ
Common questions
Is ZangFu HIPAA compliant?
ZangFu is not currently a HIPAA-certified platform. Do not enter patient names or other identifying information. Use initials or patient IDs only. We are actively working toward compliance for a future version.
How does voice dictation work?
Tap the microphone button and speak your findings aloud — tongue, pulse, symptoms, history, anything clinical. ZangFu transcribes in real time and extracts structured fields automatically. Works in Chrome and Safari. No setup required.
What are the ICD-10 suggestions?
After generating a note, one click suggests the most relevant ICD-10-CM billing codes based on your chief complaint and pattern diagnosis. Always verify before submitting a claim — these are a starting point, not a substitute for coder review.
What is the patient visit summary?
One click generates a warm, plain-language version of the visit — what was found, what was treated, herbal and dietary guidance, and next steps. Print it and hand it to the patient. No other tool generates this automatically from a TCM note.
Does it integrate with my EHR?
Not yet. ZangFu generates notes, ICD-10 codes, and patient summaries you copy and paste into any EHR. Direct integration is on the roadmap.
What styles of TCM does it support?
ZangFu is grounded in TCM pattern differentiation as taught in accredited programs across North America. It supports tongue and pulse-based diagnosis, zang-fu pattern theory, channel theory, herbal medicine, and integrative assessment including orthopedic and hara findings.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel anytime from your billing portal and you will not be charged again. Your access continues through the end of the billing period.
What is the Founding Member rate?
The first 20 subscribers lock in $19.99/month for life — even as the price increases for new users. This is our way of rewarding early adopters who help shape the product. Once 20 spots are filled, this rate is gone permanently.

Our Story

It was 1976. Steve Wozniak and I were huddled in a garage in Los Altos, soldering circuit boards by lamplight. We had no money, no investors, and a lot of bad coffee.

(None of that is true. I was not in that garage. I wasn't born until the 80s. And I've never met Steve Wozniak.)

Here is the truth. I'm an acupuncturist. I love this field. And I sit on a state regulatory board reviewing clinical notes.

After a year of that, I got annoyed. Not annoyed with the medicine — annoyed watching a sophisticated, rigorous discipline get represented by documentation that didn't come close to capturing the depth of thought behind it.

So I built something about it.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 2026

What we collect. When you start a free trial, we collect your email address. We also collect basic usage data — how many notes you generate and when. We do not collect patient information. Our HIPAA disclaimer instructs users not to enter any patient-identifying information into ZangFu.

How we use it. Your email is used to communicate product updates, feature announcements, and subscription information. We do not sell, rent, or share your email or usage data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Clinical data. Notes you generate are processed by Anthropic's Claude API. Clinical content is transmitted to Anthropic's servers to generate your note and is not stored by ZangFu beyond your browser's local storage. Patient records you create within ZangFu are stored locally in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

Cookies and storage. ZangFu uses your browser's local storage to save your trial status, email, and patient records. No tracking cookies are used. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Data retention. Trial signup emails are retained until you request deletion. To request deletion of your data, use the contact form at zangfu.app.
